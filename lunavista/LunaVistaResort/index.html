About Luna Vista Resort

Luna Vista Resort is a quiet and relaxing place in Calamba, Laguna, perfect for a family or friends getaway. It is close to the natural hot springs of Pansol, where you can enjoy swimming pools and other resort amenities. The resort offers comfortable rooms and friendly service, making your stay enjoyable and memorable.
